Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Cal

How does Cal compare to Calendly for Google Calendar scheduling?

Unlike Calendly, Cal doesn't require recipients to click links or visit websites. It works entirely through email by reading conversation threads and coordinating directly with participants. This maintains the personal touch of email while automating the logistics. Calendly forces people to interact with a separate tool, which creates friction and a 40% drop-off rate.

Does Cal work with Google Calendar?

Yes, Cal is designed specifically for Google Calendar integration. It automatically checks your availability, proposes meeting times based on your calendar, and creates Google Calendar events for all participants once a time is confirmed. Everything syncs seamlessly without any manual data entry.

How much does Cal cost for appointment scheduling?

Cal Autobot is free. In the future, we'll have more advance features for a small monthly subscription.

Is Cal better than manual scheduling for online meeting coordination?

Cal saves sales teams an average of 5+ hours per week compared to manual scheduling. It eliminates the back-and-forth email chains ("Does Tuesday at 2pm work?" "How about Wednesday?") while maintaining the personal touch that scheduling links lack. You get the efficiency of automation with the warmth of direct communication.

How quickly can I start using Cal for appointment scheduling?

Setup takes about 30 seconds. Once you have access, you simply CC Cal@CalAutobot.com on any scheduling email thread. Cal reads the conversation context and begins coordinating immediately. No complex configuration, no calendar permissions to manage, no training required.

What makes Cal different from other AI scheduling assistants?

Cal focuses on maintaining natural, human-like email conversations. Unlike scheduling links that feel transactional, Cal participates in email threads just like a real executive assistant would. It understands context, handles time zone confusion, and adapts to conversational scheduling requests rather than forcing people into rigid booking flows.
