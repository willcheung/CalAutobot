Why We Created This

As a parent of two kids, I was drowning in stream of school events, afterschool activities, and endless emails from teachers, coaches, and school portals. Every week meant manually copying dates from different places and frantically adding events to our family calendar.

There had to be a better way. That's why we built Calendar Autobot - to help busy parents, students, and anyone juggling multiple schedules get their time back.

This service is completely free because we believe everyone deserves more time for what matters most.
